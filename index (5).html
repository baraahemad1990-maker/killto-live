Kill to Live

The dead come down the street in waves. Move with WASD, aim with the mouse and hold click to fire. Every wave you survive hands you a stronger gun, and every kill pays coins to unlock new survivors. An AI partner fights beside you (the Partner button changes that).

Survivor · coins: 0 · scroll

WASD move · mouse aims, hold click to fire · R reload · P pause · L light · B autopilot plays for you. To play with a friend on one keyboard, set the start-screen Partner button to “friend”: arrow keys move, hold Space to fire. On a touch screen, left thumb moves and right thumb aims and fires.
